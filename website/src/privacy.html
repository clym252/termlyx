Termlyx Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026.

Termlyx is an SSH client. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the Termlyx apps for Android, iOS, macOS, Windows and Linux.

Summary

The developer of Termlyx does not collect, receive, store or sell any of your data. Termlyx has no user accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.

Data stored on your device

The hosts, usernames, passwords, SSH keys, snippets and settings you enter are kept on your device in an encrypted vault. The vault is encrypted with a key derived from your master password; nobody, including the developer, can read it without that password or your recovery key.

Network connections

SSH servers. Termlyx connects only to the servers you add, to open terminal, SFTP and port-forwarding sessions you start.

Sync (optional). If you turn on sync, Termlyx exchanges your vault with a sync server that you run and choose. Everything is end-to-end encrypted on your device before it is sent; the sync server only ever stores encrypted data. The developer does not operate a sync server and has no access to yours.

Apart from that, Termlyx makes no other network connections.

Permissions

Camera: only to scan the QR code that adds a device to your sync. Images are processed on the device and are never stored or sent. On Android, the QR scanner uses Google’s on-device ML Kit, which may send Google anonymous diagnostics about the scanner itself. Images and vault data are never sent.

Biometrics (fingerprint, face): optional, to unlock your vault. Biometric checks are performed by your device’s operating system; Termlyx never receives biometric data.

Notifications and foreground service (Android): used only while “Keep sessions alive” is on, to keep your SSH sessions connected in the background and show that they are running.

Location (iOS only): used only while “Keep sessions alive” is on, because iOS needs location updates to keep an app running in the background. Your location is never stored or sent anywhere.

Local network (iOS): to connect to SSH servers on your local network.

Sharing

No data is shared with the developer or with third parties.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Termlyx deletes its data from that device. Data on your own sync server is under your control; delete it there.

Children

Termlyx is a tool for system administrators and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions: use the developer contact email shown on the Termlyx page in Google Play or the App Store.

This website

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics.
